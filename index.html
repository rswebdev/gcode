Audio Wave Visualizer → G-code

Workflow

  1. Choose a Source (microphone or noise) and a Shape.
  2. Click Record — captures frames at the configured Record FPS, auto-stops at Max Frames.
  3. Orbit the 3D view with the mouse, then click Export G-code.
  4. The exported file contains the current camera view — move the camera first to frame the plot you want.

Advanced — Settings

Max FramesFrame count at which recording auto-stops (8–256).
Amp ScaleVertical exaggeration of the waveform amplitude.
Speed (mm/min)Plotter feed rate — affects G-code only, not the preview.
Pen ModeZ axis — uses G0/G1 Z moves to lift/lower the pen (stepper motor). Servo (M3) — uses M3 S{value} to drive a servo connected to the spindle PWM output. Use Servo mode if your Z doesn't respond to Z commands.
Pen Up Z(Z axis mode) Z height (mm) when the pen is lifted. Try 5; use negative values if your Z-axis is inverted.
Pen Down Z(Z axis mode) Z height (mm) when the pen touches paper. Try 0 or a small negative value.
Servo Up S(Servo mode) S value for pen lifted. With $30=1000 and 50 Hz GRBL the MG90S safe range is S50–S100 (5%–10% duty = 1–2 ms pulse). Default 80 ≈ 1.6 ms. Adjust until the servo fully lifts the pen. If the servo moves the wrong way, swap Up and Down values.
Servo Down S(Servo mode) S value for pen on paper. Default 50 ≈ 1 ms (minimum MG90S pulse). Values below S50 (with $30=1000) are below the servo's minimum and may not move it. Adjust down from 50 until the pen just touches the paper.
FFT SizeAudio analysis window size. Higher = more frequency detail.
Record FPSFrames captured per second (1–30). Lower = slower temporal evolution in the plot.
SmoothingMic frequency-mode averaging (0 = raw, 0.95 = very smooth). Hidden when using Noise source.

Send to Plotter

Requires Chrome or Edge over HTTPS / localhost. Connects directly to GRBL over USB Serial (115200 baud).

Send to PlotterOpens the port picker (first time), then streams G-code line-by-line with GRBL acknowledgement. Click again to cancel mid-job.
HomeRuns the GRBL homing cycle ($H). Requires limit switches.
Zero X,YZeros X and Y work coordinates at the current head position (G92 X0 Y0). Jog the head to your plot origin corner, then click this.
Zero ZZeros Z at the current height (G92 Z0). Jog Z down until the pen just touches the paper, then click this. Pen Down Z should then be 0 and Pen Up Z your lift amount.
Pen Up / Pen DownMove Z to the configured Pen Up / Pen Down Z values. Use to verify the lift height before plotting.
Sweep SRamps M3 S0 → S1000 → S0 in 50-step increments (300 ms per step) so you can watch the servo travel across its full range and identify the right Up/Down S values. Connects to the plotter automatically. Click again to stop mid-sweep.
JogMove the head in X, Y, or Z by the selected step size. Use to position before zeroing. Step sizes: 0.1 mm (fine), 1 mm, 10 mm.

Export

The filename is a three-word name derived from the generation parameters — the same settings always produce the same name, making plots easy to identify and re-generate. All parameters are also written as comments at the top of the G-code file.

Anaglyph export generates a two-pass file (red pen then cyan pen) for red-cyan 3D glasses.
